# Voice mix plan v2: natural "recorded live" narration + soft music (2026-11-13)

Goal: the final video should feel like Firoz and Urmy talked while they were using the app, not like a script read over a
screen recording, with a soft background music bed. Their own voices only; no pitch change, no AI voice.

Input: `Voice Clip/` (36 clips, 18 F `.m4a` + 18 U `.aac`), `submission/presentation/DRMS_Group3_Demo_Video_silent.mp4` (11:21),
`video_map.json`. Output: `submission/presentation/DRMS_Group3_Demo_Video.mp4` (1080p30, AAC 192 kb/s, about 11:25).
Originals are never modified; working files go to `Voice Clip/_work/` (git-ignored).

## Analysis already done (v1)

All 36 present, speakers match, no clipping, no missing sentences. Firoz is about 10 dB quieter than Urmy; Urmy's files are
noisier and her phone's two mics are uncorrelated, so the cleaner single channel is used per clip; 29_F is 1.7 s longer than its slot,

## Why it sounds "read" and what fixes each cause

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| Every line starts on the scene cut, like a slide narration | Place each sentence against the on-screen action it describes (step 3) |
| Two different rooms/phones: listeners hear "two separate recordings" | Match both voices to one tone and put them in one shared small room (step 2) |
| Dead silence between lines (studio-like) | Continuous room tone + keep natural breaths |
| Long, even reading pauses inside lines | Shorten pauses over 0.7 s, keep short ones; vary the gaps between lines |
| Nothing in the audio "happens" when the cursor clicks or types | Quiet mouse clicks and keyboard sounds on the real clicks and typing |
| Flat reading intonation | Cannot be changed by processing (honest limit); the fixes above hide most of it |

## Steps

1. **Convert and clean** (each clip, WAV 48 kHz float mono):
   - best channel; trim silence (keep the in-breath before the first word);
   - high-pass 80 Hz; light `afftdn` denoise (F about 8 dB, U about 12 dB, lighter than v1 so breaths and air stay natural);
   - de-esser; gentle 2.5:1 compressor; 2-pass loudnorm so both speakers are equally loud.
2. **One room for both voices:**
   - EQ-match each speaker's average spectrum to a shared target, so Firoz and Urmy sound like the same laptop-desk setup;
   - add a very subtle small-room reverb (generated impulse response, about 0.25 s, wet about 8 percent), the same for both;
   - a continuous, very quiet room-tone bed under the whole video (no dead silence).
3. **Live timing (rough, pause-based; user chose no speech-to-text):**
   - Click times: detect the cursor's blue click ripple in the video frames, plus the typing stretches.
   - Each line is split into sentences at its pauses (gaps over 0.35 s). Sentences are matched in order to the script's sentences by length. A sentence whose script text names an action ("I save", "now I delete...") starts about 0.5 s before the nearest matching click, so the action lands while it is being said (accuracy about ±0.5 s). Other sentences follow with varied gaps of 0.25-0.8 s.
   - Some lines start 0.3-0.6 s before the scene cut, the way a person starts talking before they finish clicking.
   - Speaker hand-overs (F to U and back) get a natural 0.5-0.8 s turn gap.
   - Reading pauses over 0.7 s are shortened to about 0.45 s.
   - Where speech still does not fit (29_F, and any line that grows from action-alignment), freeze the frame for the missing time and shift the rest. `video_map.json` timecodes are updated.
4. **Desk sounds:** soft mouse click on each detected click and a soft keyboard texture during typing, about 25 dB under the voice. They are generated (no copyright) and randomly varied so they do not sound looped.
5. **Background music: "White Moon" by BigRicePiano** (https://youtu.be/IBepaE3eFeM), chosen by the user:
   - source file: `Voice Clip/background music.mpeg` (MP3 160 kb/s, 48 kHz stereo, 4:16 incl. 3.2 s silence at the end, -20.8 LUFS, has an embedded cover image that is ignored);
   - the video is about 11:25, so the track plays about 2.7 times: trim the silent tail, loop with a 4-6 s crossfade between a calm ending phrase and a calm phrase after the intro;
   - kept very quiet: about 28 dB under the voice while someone speaks (sidechain ducking), about 18 dB under during gaps longer than 2 s, so speech is always perfectly clear;
   - softened: low-pass around 6 kHz plus a dip at 1-3 kHz, so the piano never competes with the voice;
   - about 3 s intro before the first word, rising slightly at the 3 part changes, fading out over the last 4 s;
   - if the track is shorter than 11:25, it loops with a long crossfade at a calm bar, with no hard cuts;
   - credit line for the YouTube description: "Music: White Moon by BigRicePiano". It goes in the description only, since the video has no on-screen text. Check the uploaded video for a Content ID claim.
6. **Video polish (no on-screen text):** 0.5 s fade from black at the start, 1.5 s hold + fade to black at the end; one re-encode (x264 CRF 18, `+faststart`).
7. **Checkpoint sample:** render a 60 s sample (hello, then a CRUD stretch with clicks and typing, then a speaker hand-over). **Stop and wait for your OK** (music volume, reverb amount, click loudness) before the full render.
8. **Full render and verify:**
   - final loudness -14 LUFS (±1), true peak at most -1.5 dBTP, length within 10:00-12:00;
   - sync check: frame grabs at 8 action sentences;
   - you get before/after clips;
   - YouTube chapter list from the new timecodes.
